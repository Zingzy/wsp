# Leads and sub-threads

An agent in one thread can start other threads, on your Mac or on another computer. A Claude Code thread can start a Codex thread to review its branch, and read what it found. The thread at the top of such a tree is the lead, and the threads it started are its sub-threads. Every one shows in your sidebar.

<Image src="/shots/sidebar.webp" alt="A lead's row in the sidebar with its three working sub-threads under it, and its Finished fold open on five more" caption="A lead in the sidebar, with the threads it started under it." />

## How an agent starts a sub-thread

An agent starts a sub-thread with `wsp run`, or the `run` tool of the [wsp tools](/agents/tools), and names itself as the thread to tell when it ends:

```sh
wsp run lab --agent codex --notify me "Review the branch fix/login and list what is wrong."
```

Inside a thread, `me` is that thread. In your own terminal, `me` is you.

When the sub-thread's turn ends, wsp sends each target one line as a message:

```txt
thread 1a2b3c4d finished (completed, 12m 4s, $0.41): <last line of the reply>
```

A thread also gets the whole final reply, and each finished line starts its next turn. So a lead does not wait or poll. See [Coordinating threads from an agent](/agents/coordinating).

## The tree in the sidebar

Sub-threads hang under their lead's tile, the live ones first.

Finished sub-threads go into a **Finished** fold under the live ones. A subagent, the agent's own helper inside one turn, shows as a slim row with a bot icon. `wsp threads --tree` prints the same tree in a terminal.

## A sub-thread inside the lead's transcript

Each sub-thread also shows as a tile in the lead's transcript, where the lead started it, with its status and its question, failure or last line. Click it to open the sub-thread.

Above the composer, the **Threads** section lists the whole tree. Each row has **Send a message**, to write into that thread from the lead's page.

## Settle and restore

To settle a thread is to fold it, and every thread under it, into the **Settled** section at the foot of the sidebar. Nothing is deleted. A settled thread comes back by itself on its next turn.

- On a quiet tile, click the settle button.
- Right-click a **Finished** fold and choose **Settle N finished**.
- Drag a lead's tile onto **Settled**.
- Leave it. **Settings > General > Settle a thread after** settles a thread you have read once it is quiet for that long.

A notice then offers **Undo**.

From a terminal:

```sh
wsp thread settle 1a2b3c4d
wsp thread settle 1a2b3c4d --finished
wsp thread restore 1a2b3c4d
```

`--finished` settles the finished threads under the one you name, but not that thread, and not a failed one. `wsp thread restore` brings a thread back out of **Settled**.

## Stop a tree

When you stop a thread, every running thread under it stops too. `wsp stop --subagent <id>` stops one subagent alone, and the turn goes on.

## Restart a thread that failed

`wsp run --replaces <thread>` starts a new thread in place of a stopped or failed one. wsp settles the old thread once the new one starts. The new thread's menu offers **Open the thread it replaced**.
