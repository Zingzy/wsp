# The switcher and the palette

{/* Written from apps/web/src/keybindingDefaults.ts, apps/web/src/shell/ (shellCommands.ts, threadHistory.ts, workspaceSwitcher.ts), apps/web/src/components/palette/ and apps/web/src/keybindings.ts. */}

Three ways move you between threads with the keyboard. The switcher steps through the threads you opened last. The palette searches threads and actions. The jump keys open a row of the palette's Tasks group by its number.

In the chords below, `mod` is `Cmd` on a Mac and `Ctrl` elsewhere. You can change every chord in Settings > Keybindings. The [keyboard shortcuts](/features/shortcuts) page lists them all.

## The switcher

Each card in the switcher shows a thread: a preview of its page, its title, its agent, its computer and its state.

<Image src="/shots/switcher.webp" alt="The switcher held open over five threads." />

1. Hold `Ctrl` and press `Tab`. The next card is picked.
2. Press `Tab` again to move on, or `Shift+Tab` to move back.
3. Let go of `Ctrl` to open the picked thread.

Press `Esc` to close the switcher without a move. A quick `Ctrl+Tab` takes you back to the thread you had open before, so you can go back and forth between two threads.

The cards run from the threads you opened most recently. Settled threads are left out.

`mod+alt+left` and `mod+alt+right` open the same switcher. `mod+alt+up` and `mod+alt+down` open it over the open project's threads only.

When the right panel has focus and more than one tab, `Ctrl+Tab` steps through its tabs instead. A focused terminal keeps `Ctrl+Tab` for itself.

## The palette

Press `mod+k` or `mod+shift+p`, or click **Search** at the top of the sidebar. Before you type, the palette shows three groups:

- **Actions**, such as **New thread**, **Add a project**, **Settings** and **Report a bug**, and the open project's actions.
- **Tasks**, one row for each project folder and each worktree that has a thread, with its jump key.
- **Recent threads**, the threads started last.

When you type, it searches the actions, the Tasks rows, your thread titles and the words in their messages. A match in a message shows under **In messages**. Start with `>` to search the actions only. An action that cannot run now shows grey, with the reason.

The palette does not search files. Press `mod+p` for that. See [Files](/features/files).

## Jump to a row

`mod+1` to `mod+9` open the rows of the palette's **Tasks** group by their number. The rows stay in the order wsp made them, oldest first. The sidebar shows threads, not these rows, so the numbers do not match the sidebar. Open the palette to see which row has which number.

## Go to the next thread that needs you

Press `mod+alt+u` to open the next thread that waits for you. A thread waits for you when it asks a question or a permission, or when it finished or failed and you have not seen it yet.

## In a browser tab

A browser keeps some chords for itself: `Ctrl+Tab`, `mod` with a number, `mod+t`, and on a Mac `Cmd+Option` with the arrows. In a browser tab, use the palette or the sidebar instead.
