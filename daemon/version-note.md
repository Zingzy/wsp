A turn's changed-files range is now what the agent itself changed: each commit it wrote contributes its own files, collected one by one over the turn's reflog window whatever moved HEAD after them, together with the edits standing in its end worktree and the files it resolved by hand in a merge, while a HEAD move it did not write (a checkout, pull, merge, rebase or reset) is named on a line with no files of its own. A rebase is one such line plus the edits standing at the end; the commits it replayed and any conflict it resolved mid-rebase are not listed.
