Starts and reviews from a link: git.issueRead reads an issue, git.prCheckout puts a copy on a pull request's head through the host's command line, git.prDiff reads a diff cut on a file's boundary, and git.prReview posts one review whole with a comment outside the diff put into its body. A pull request read names its author and its fork, a bring back pushes where the branch's own configuration points, and git.pr fills under a title or a body given.
